HR Workforce Dashboard

A browser replica of the Power BI report in this project. It reads the same Excel data and applies the same measure definitions, so the numbers match what the report should show. Layout and styling are close, not identical.

Try it: change the Year or Month slicer, click a bar to cross-filter the other visuals, switch a time chart between Quarter and Month, or double-click a location bar to open its country profile.
